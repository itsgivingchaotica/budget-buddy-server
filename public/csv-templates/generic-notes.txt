Budget Buddy - generic CSV format (PO-18)

One file = one account. You choose the account when you upload.

File: UTF-8 text, comma-separated, with a header row. Values containing a
comma go in double quotes, e.g. "Rent - Example Property, LLC".
Limits: 2 MB and 10,000 rows per file.

Columns (any order; names are not case-sensitive):
  date            required. YYYY-MM-DD, or month first: M/D/YYYY or
                  MM/DD/YYYY (e.g. 2/3/2026, 02/03/2026 = 3 February 2026).
                  The year must have 4 digits. Day-first dates
                  (e.g. 31/12/2026) are not accepted. Use the posted date.
  description     required. Up to 255 characters.
  amount          required. Negative = money out of the account,
                  positive = money in. "$", commas and (12.34) for -12.34
                  are allowed; at most 2 decimals. US dollars only.
                  Credit cards: purchases negative, payments to the card
                  positive. Rows with 0.00 are skipped.
  transaction_id  optional. Your bank's ID for the row; used to spot
                  duplicates when present.
  balance         optional. Running balance after the row.

Other columns are ignored. Leave out pending transactions: export only
posted ones.

Change log
  2026-11-24  Dates may use one-digit month and day (M/D/YYYY) as well as
              MM/DD/YYYY. Two-digit years and day-first dates are still
              rejected.
